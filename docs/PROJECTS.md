# Projects

A short index of the repositories I actively build on. Everything here runs on real operations,
not demos.

## Product and platform work

- **[aimscams](https://github.com/atilioobadia-cpu/aimscams)** — back office for Tanzanian SACCOs
  and microfinance institutions: members and KYC, savings and shares, credit, teller operations,
  double-entry ledger, regulatory returns. `TypeScript` `Next.js` `NestJS` `PostgreSQL`
- **[warehouse_management](https://github.com/atilioobadia-cpu/warehouse_management)** — warehouse
  management system for X Electronics: items, stock movements and locations. `Python` `Frappe`
- **[erpnext_payroll_dashboard](https://github.com/atilioobadia-cpu/erpnext_payroll_dashboard)** —
  payroll dashboard for ERPNext: salary components, payroll runs and settlement. `Python` `Frappe`
- **[AIMS_Ai_Agent](https://github.com/atilioobadia-cpu/AIMS_Ai_Agent)** — LLM-powered assistant
  for the AIMS ecosystem, embedded in the desk. `TypeScript`
- **[BDSTanzania](https://github.com/atilioobadia-cpu/BDSTanzania)** — corporate website for the
  Business Development Society of Tanzania. `HTML` `CSS`
- **[POS-System](https://github.com/atilioobadia-cpu/POS-System)** — point-of-sale application.
  `Python`
